# Dynamics

## Newton's laws of motion

1. First Law (Law of Inertia) : An object stays at rest or keeps moving at a constant velocity in a straight line unless acted on by a net external force.
2. Second Law (F = ma) : Net force equals mass times acceleration, or the time rate of change of momentum. Acceleration is directly proportional to net force and inversely proportional to mass.
3. Third Law (Action and Reaction) : When one object exerts a force on a second object, the second exerts a force of equal magnitude and opposite direction on the first.
