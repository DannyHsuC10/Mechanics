# Mechanical Principle

These notes describe how machine elements transmit motion, carry loads, and deliver power. The gear section includes geometric derivations and plotting code; later sections cover other components and manufacturing.

## Gear

A gear also called a toothed wheel, is a rotating machine part typically used to transmit rotational motion or torque by means of a series of "teeth" that engage with compatible teeth of another gear or other part.
